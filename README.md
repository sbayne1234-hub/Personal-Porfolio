# My Little Universe
My Little Universe is a personal portfolio website. This website shows a little bit of my life, interests, accomplishments, and ideas. The website includes information about me, my skills and hobbies, my milestones, and some projects and creations I've worked on.  

It includes:
  1. About Me or "My Journey": About my personal interests
  2. Projects or "My Creations": Different projects ive made
  3. accomplishments or "My Milestones": things that ive done that have changed me for the better
  4. skills or "My Little Universe of Skills": skills and hobbies
  5. contact (to be named): my email
  6. space themed design with falling stars in background

How to run:
  1. click the demo link
  2. once loaded explore the home page to learn a little about me and my interests
  3. visit the creations or milestones page to go more in depth into those sections. If clicking on the links in the creations page, they could bring you to a new page and you would have to use the back arrow to go back to the portfolio.
